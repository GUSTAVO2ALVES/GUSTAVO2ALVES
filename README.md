# Gustavo Alves
### Data Engineer · Technical Leadership · Business Intelligence

I connect business needs with reliable data solutions, from ingestion and transformation to analytical models and dashboards.

Based in Curitiba, Brazil. My background spans data engineering, analytics, product, and project management. I combine hands-on development with technical guidance, prioritization, and collaboration across technical and business teams.

[Connect on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gustavo-alves-201715131/)

## What I work on

- **Data pipelines:** ingestion and transformation with Python and SQL, orchestrated with Apache Airflow.
- **Data platforms:** development and maintenance of analytical environments using AWS and PostgreSQL.
- **Data reliability:** validation, reconciliation, incremental loading strategies, and investigation of inconsistencies.
- **SQL performance:** query review, data modeling, and optimization of materialized views for analytical workloads.
- **Business Intelligence:** dashboards and KPIs with clear business definitions and attention to usability and performance.
- **Technical leadership:** translating requirements, organizing priorities, supporting engineering decisions, and evaluating architecture alternatives.
